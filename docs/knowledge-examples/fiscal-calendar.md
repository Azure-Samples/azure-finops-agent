# Example: Fiscal calendar

> **Category:** Custom
> Copy this into a new Knowledge Base article and adjust the dates. This lets the
> agent align cost reports and budgets to your fiscal year instead of the
> calendar year.

## Fiscal year definition

- Fiscal year runs **1 July → 30 June**.
- **FY26** = 1 Jul 2025 → 30 Jun 2026.
- Quarters:
  - Q1: Jul–Sep
  - Q2: Oct–Dec
  - Q3: Jan–Mar
  - Q4: Apr–Jun

## Budget cycle

- Annual budgets are set in **May**, effective from the 1 July start.
- Mid-year re-forecast happens at the **end of Q2** (December).
- Quarterly business reviews fall in the first week after each quarter close.

## How to use this

- When I say "this quarter" or "YTD", use the **fiscal** calendar above, not the
  calendar year.
- Align budget-vs-actual and forecast reports to the fiscal year.
- For QBR-style summaries, compare the closed quarter to the same quarter last
  fiscal year.
